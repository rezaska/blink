Blink

Privacy Policy · Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Blink is a macOS menu-bar app that gives you gentle, ambient reminders to blink, to help prevent dry and tired eyes. It is built so that all of your data stays on your own device, under your control. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no tracking. Blink makes no network connections at all.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal information, accounts, or identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking.
  • No webcam images or video. If you turn on the optional webcam mode, video frames are analysed on your device, in memory, and are never saved, shown, or transmitted.
  • Nothing leaves your device.

What the app stores

Blink stores your preferences (your chosen cue, sensitivity, and detection mode) and simple local usage counts (such as how many reminders were shown) in a plain settings file on your Mac. This information stays on your device and is never uploaded.

Permissions used

  • Camera (optional). Requested only if you enable webcam detection. It is used solely on your device to detect blinks, and is never recorded or transmitted. Blink works fully without the camera in Timer mode.

Your control over your data

  • The webcam is off by default; you choose whether to enable it.
  • You can export your settings and stats to a file at any time from Settings.
  • You can delete all of your data from Settings, which returns the app to its first-run state.

Changes to this policy
