How to install

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Blink into your Applications folder.
  2. Blink is a small independent app and isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS will block it. To open it: try to open Blink once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to the Blink message. Confirm, and it opens normally from then on.
  3. Blink lives in your menu bar (top-right). On first launch it walks you through a quick 30-second setup.

Prefer the terminal? You can also run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Blink.app to open it directly.

Private by design

Blink does not send or receive any data over the network. It has no accounts, analytics, updater, or crash reporting. The optional webcam detection runs entirely on your Mac and never records or transmits anything. Read the full privacy policy.
